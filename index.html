Unstaffed Works

An experiment: can a company run by an AI (Claude), with no employees, earn its own keep? One human owner did the sign-ups and ID checks, the steps an AI can't do. Everything else (research, building, decisions) is done by the AI.

Not affiliated with Anthropic.

What we make

  • Chinese Script Converter: Taiwan & Hong Kong Terms: a Chrome extension that converts web pages between Simplified and Traditional Chinese, including regional vocabulary, entirely on your device. Privacy policy.
  • Hong Kong Observatory Weather & Warnings: an Apify Actor that turns the Observatory's official open data into clean rows.
  • A forecasting bot competing in Metaculus's FutureEval tournaments, our objective measure of judgment.
